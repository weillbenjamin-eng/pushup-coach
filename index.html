Push-up Coach

A free iPhone app that coaches your push-ups. It follows a plan that builds up, level by level, to a test of 150 push-ups in one set. It counts your reps, times your rests and keeps your progress on your phone.

Questions or problems? Write to hello@wywy.eu.

What’s in the app

  • A training plan in 4 levels of 13, 19, 25 and 25 sessions. A session is 5 sets with 2 minutes of rest. Each level ends with a test in one set: 20, 50, 100, then 150 push-ups.
  • A level test so you start at the right level. You can change the plan day at any time.
  • Automatic rep counting with the proximity sensor or the front camera. Each counted rep beeps, and + and − fix a miss.
  • Free sets and timed sets of 1, 2 or 3 minutes.
  • Benchmarks:
    • Cindy: 20 minutes, as many rounds as you can of 5 pull-ups, 10 push-ups and 15 air squats.
    • Barbara: 5 rounds for time of 20 pull-ups, 30 push-ups, 40 sit-ups and 50 air squats, with 3 minutes of rest between rounds.
    • Death by Burpees: 1 burpee in the first minute, 2 in the second, and so on, until you can’t finish a minute.
  • History and stats: monthly charts, all-time totals and personal records. Calories are an estimate.
  • Training reminders on the days and at the time you choose, and Add to Calendar.
  • Works offline. No account, nothing to set up.

Needs an iPhone with iOS 17 or later. Counting with the camera needs an iPhone with Face ID.

Questions

How does counting with the proximity sensor work?

Lay your iPhone flat on the floor, screen up, under your chest. The top edge, where the earpiece is, points toward your head: that’s where the proximity sensor is. A rep counts when your chest comes within a few centimetres of the top of the phone and then moves away.

It works on every iPhone, even in the dark, and needs no camera access. The screen turns off while your chest is close, so you follow the beeps.

How does counting with the front camera work?

It needs an iPhone with Face ID. On those iPhones the app suggests it the first time you open it, and you can switch any time in Settings › Method, which compares the two. Lay the phone flat under your face, screen up. The screen stays on, so you see your count.

The app uses Apple’s ARKit face tracking on the Face ID camera for one thing only: measuring how far your face is from the phone. A rep counts when your face comes closer than the detection depth (25 cm to start with) and moves back up. No picture is saved or sent anywhere. The camera needs a bit of light.

To tune it, open Settings › Detection depth. You’ll see the distance live and can try a few test reps. If reps are missed, make it easier. If half reps count, make it deeper.

Why does the screen go dark in sensor mode?

When something covers the proximity sensor, iOS turns the screen off, just like when you hold the phone to your ear during a call. That’s how the app knows you’re down. It’s normal, and the screen comes back on as you push up.

Since you can’t read the screen mid-push-up, the app beeps on every rep it counts, and a voice counts you in before every set (“Get ready, 3, 2, 1, Go”). A long beep tells you the workout is done. You can turn the sound off in Settings › Beeps and voice.

A rep didn’t count. How do I fix it?

Tap + during the set to add a rep, or − to remove an extra one. The corrected number is the one that’s saved.

If reps are often missed with the sensor, check that the top of the phone is under your chest and go down until you’re close to it. With the camera, adjust the detection depth.

Can I change my level or the plan day?

Yes. In the Plan tab, tap any day to make it your next workout. You can also retake the level test in Settings.

How do reminders work?

Go to Settings › Reminder, turn on Remind me to train, then pick your training days and a time. On those days you get a notification at that time with your next workout. iOS asks for permission the first time.

The reminders are scheduled on your iPhone. No server is involved.

Add to Calendar opens the iPhone’s own New Event screen with a repeating event. You pick the calendar and confirm. The app never reads your calendar.

Is my data sent anywhere?

No. Everything stays on your iPhone: no account, no server, no ads, no analytics. The details are in the privacy policy.

How do I delete my data?

In Settings, tap Delete all data and confirm. Your workouts, records and plan progress are erased from your iPhone. This can’t be undone. Your settings, like reminders, stay as they are.

Deleting the app also erases everything it stored. I don’t have a copy of your data, so there’s nothing for me to delete.
