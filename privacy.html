Privacy policy

Effective date: October 2026

In short

  • The app collects no personal data and sends nothing to me. Settings › About shows this website, hosted by GitHub, which logs your IP address (section 8).
  • Your workouts and settings are stored only on your iPhone.
  • If you choose it, the front camera only measures how far your face is from the phone. No image or face data is saved or sent.
  • No account, no ads, no analytics, no tracking. The website has no cookies.

1. Who is responsible

Push-up Coach (the iPhone app and this website) is published by its developer, a private individual based in France (“I”, “me”). My name is the one shown as the seller on the app’s App Store page, and I’ll give it to you by email if you ask.

The only personal data I may receive is what you send me by email. For that data, I am the controller under the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR). For any question about this policy or your data, write to hello@wywy.eu.

2. What the app stores on your iPhone

The app keeps the following in its own storage on your iPhone, so that it works. None of it is sent to me or to anyone else: the app has no server and no account, and I never have access to it.

  • Your workouts: date and time, type of workout, reps per set, how they were counted (sensor, camera or by hand), duration and estimated calories. Used to show your history, stats and personal records.
  • Your plan: your level and day, and your result in the level test (max reps in a row). Used to pick your next workout.
  • Your weight, if you enter it in Settings. Used only to estimate calories.
  • Your settings: counting method, detection depth, sound, count aloud, beep sound, voice, rest between sets, units, reminder days and time. Used to remember your choices.

iCloud sync is not used. Like any app’s data, it may be included in your own iPhone or iCloud backups, which are managed by you and Apple under Apple’s terms.

How long it’s kept: until you delete it. In the app, Settings › Delete all data erases your workouts, your plan progress, your level test result and your weight. Your settings, including reminders, stay until you change them. Deleting the app erases everything it stored on your iPhone.

3. Camera and face data

This section applies only if you count your reps with the front camera.

  • When: only if you choose Front camera as the counting method in Settings, and only while a workout is on screen or you are on the Detection depth screen. The camera stops as soon as you leave the workout or that screen. iOS asks for your permission first. You can take it back at any time in the iPhone’s Settings app; the proximity sensor then counts instead.
  • What is read: the app uses Apple’s ARKit face tracking with the Face ID (TrueDepth) camera. From each camera frame, it reads only the position of your face, to work out its distance from the phone in centimetres.
  • Why: to count a rep when your face comes close to the phone and moves back up. That is the only use.
  • What happens to it: each frame is handled on your iPhone, in real time, and dropped right away. No photo, video, face map, depth map or face measurement is stored, and nothing leaves your iPhone.
  • What it is never used for: face data is never used to identify you, nor for advertising, marketing, profiling, data mining or any other purpose. It is never shared with anyone, including third parties.

The app doesn’t use Face ID to unlock anything and has no access to your Face ID data.

4. Proximity sensor

With the proximity sensor, the app only learns whether something is close to the top of the screen or not. It reads this on your iPhone during a workout to count reps. Nothing is stored or sent, and no permission is needed.

5. Notifications

Training reminders are local notifications: the app schedules them on your iPhone through iOS. There is no push server and no data is sent. You can turn them off in the app or in the iPhone’s Settings app.

6. Calendar

Add to Calendar opens the iPhone’s own New Event screen. You choose the calendar and confirm. The app gets no access to your calendar and can’t read your events.

7. Emails to support

If you write to hello@wywy.eu, I receive your email address and your message, plus anything you attach.

  • Purpose: to answer you.
  • Legal basis: my legitimate interest in answering questions about the app (GDPR, Article 6(1)(f)).
  • How long: I delete the conversation 12 months after our last exchange.
  • Storage: my mailbox is hosted by OVHcloud (OVH SAS, France), which keeps it on my behalf as my processor under the GDPR. The mailbox is stored in data centres in the European Union.
  • Access from outside the EU: my contract with OVHcloud allows companies in its group, including one based in Canada, to access the mailbox remotely, only when needed to provide and secure the service. Canada is covered by an adequacy decision of the European Commission. For access from a country without such a decision, the contract uses the European Commission’s standard contractual clauses, and I can send you a copy on request. To learn more, see OVHcloud’s personal data protection page.
  • Sharing: none, apart from my email host above. I don’t sell your emails or pass them on to anyone else.

8. No cookies, analytics or tracking

The app has no ads, no analytics and no third-party code, and it doesn’t track you. If you allowed it in your iPhone settings (Privacy & Security › Analytics & Improvements › Share With App Developers), Apple may send me crash reports and usage statistics that don’t identify you.

This website uses no cookies, no analytics and no external fonts or scripts. It is hosted by GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc.). When you visit it, GitHub logs technical data such as your IP address, for security. GitHub handles that data under the GitHub General Privacy Statement. I don’t receive these logs. Inside the app, Settings › About shows these same pages, and GitHub logs those visits in the same way.

9. Children

Push-up Coach is not directed at children under 15. Since the app collects no personal data, it collects none from children either. If your child has emailed me, write to me and I’ll delete the email.

10. Your rights

Under the GDPR and French law, you have the right to access, correct and erase your personal data, to restrict or object to its processing, to data portability, and to set instructions for what happens to your data after your death.

For the data in the app, you exercise these rights directly: it is only on your iPhone, you can see it in the app, and you can erase it with Delete all data or by deleting the app. I hold no copy, so I can’t access or erase it for you.

For your emails, write to hello@wywy.eu. I’ll answer within one month.

You can also file a complaint with the French data protection authority, the CNIL (cnil.fr), or the authority in the EU country where you live.

11. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, I’ll update this page and its date. If a future version of the app ever processes personal data in a new way (for example with ads or purchases), this policy and the App Store privacy details will be updated before that version is released.
